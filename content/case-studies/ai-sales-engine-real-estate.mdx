## Good leads, slow first replies

The agency handles sales, leasing and property management. Enquiries arrive all day, from listing portals, the website, ads, referrals and the phone. Each one needs a fast answer, a few qualifying questions and, if it's serious, a viewing or a meeting.

That work landed on the agents. Every enquiry had to be read, called back, qualified and booked by hand, between inspections, appraisals and negotiations. Leads that arrived after hours or on a busy afternoon waited. Managers could see the deals, but not the leads going cold behind them.

### A typical afternoon, before

- Three portal enquiries arrive while an agent is at an inspection.
- A call-back happens hours later. The buyer has already spoken to someone else.
- An agent spends 20 minutes with a prospect who was never going to buy.
- A tenant's question sits in an inbox overnight.
- The manager finds out a deal went cold at the weekly meeting.

## Our approach: AI makes the first move, agents close

We didn't set out to replace agents. We set out to remove the work that keeps them from talking to serious buyers, sellers and tenants: the call-backs, the sorting, the scheduling and the routine questions.

The rule for every feature was the same: the AI does the first touch and the admin, and a person takes over the moment judgement or a relationship matters.

## The lead flow: from first message to closed sale

Every lead follows the same path. The AI agents handle the first contact, the booking and the follow-up; the agent owns the visit and the deal; leadership watches it all happen live and can step in at any point.

1. **Lead captured.** The buyer enquires through an ad, portal, website, social, referral, webinar or outreach. The lead is tagged by source and appears on the master dashboard.
2. **WhatsApp agent messages first.** Small talk, answers to any question 24/7, and a lead score from 0 to 100. Leadership can read any chat and step in.
3. **Voice agent calls.** It picks up from the chat, asks what time suits and books the site visit. The call transcript and score are saved.
4. **The visit is assigned.** By intent, score, who's free and the client profile; high-value sites go to senior agents. The visit lands in the agent's Google Calendar.
5. **Reminders and confirmations.** A reminder the day before and a confirmation that morning. The buyer can reschedule at any time on WhatsApp, and the agent gets a heads-up.
6. **Site visit.** The buyer and the assigned agent meet at the property.
7. **Feedback.** The AI calls the buyer for feedback and asks the agent how serious the buyer is.
8. **The agent works the deal** in their own dashboard and marks the sale won. Sale and revenue update live for leadership.

## Module: the call that books the visit

When a buyer is ready, the voice agent calls. It picks up where the WhatsApp chat left off, asks what time suits them for a site visit, and books it on the call. Every call ends with a clear outcome and a recommended next step, and the full transcript is saved to the lead's profile.

### The call script

1. **Opening.** Greets the buyer by name and picks up from the WhatsApp chat.
2. **Motivation.** Why they're looking to buy, sell, rent or move.
3. **Must-haves.** Location, bedrooms, parking, outdoor space.
4. **Budget.** A comfortable price range.
5. **Finance.** Pre-approval status.
6. **Timing.** Asks which day and time suit for a site visit.
7. **Close.** Books the visit, or sends a shortlist or schedules a call-back.

### Every call ends with one of five outcomes

| Outcome | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Site visit booked | Time agreed on the call, visit booked, reminders scheduled |
| Qualified | Shortlist sent, lead assigned to an agent |
| Follow-up needed | Call-back scheduled, market update sent |
| Unqualified | Closed politely, preferences kept on file |
| No answer | Logged, ready for the next attempt |

## Module: every lead scored, summarised and routed

Each lead gets a score from 0 to 100, built from four factors, plus an AI-written summary. An agent opening a lead knows in seconds whether it's worth a call, and what to say.

On a demo lead profile, an overall score of 87 breaks down into budget fit 89, urgency 85, engagement 75 and intent 98. The AI summary lists the customer's goals, pain points and buying intent, followed by recommended next actions such as sharing a neighbourhood market report, booking a viewing and connecting with a financing partner.

## Module: every visit booked, confirmed and covered

Once a visit is booked on the call, the engine keeps it on track: the right agent assigned, reminders on both sides, and a feedback loop afterwards. The buyer can reschedule at any time, and both calendars follow.

1. **Booked:** time agreed on the AI call.
2. **Assigned:** right agent, synced to their calendar.
3. **Day before:** reminder to the buyer, heads-up to the agent.
4. **That morning:** buyer confirms; agent reminded.
5. **Site visit:** agent meets the buyer.
6. **Feedback:** AI calls the buyer; asks the agent.

| Who gets the visit | How it's decided |
| --- | --- |
| Intent and score | How serious the buyer is, from the chat and the call |
| Availability | Which agents are free at the agreed time |
| Client profile | What the buyer is looking for and where |
| Property value | High-value sites go to the most experienced agents |

| What each side gets | |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer | Reminder the day before, confirmation that morning, reschedule any time on WhatsApp |
| Agent | Visit in Google Calendar, heads-up the day before and on the day, with the lead's summary |
| Leadership | Every visit, reschedule and confirmation on the master dashboard |

After the visit, the AI calls the buyer for feedback and asks the agent how serious the buyer is. From there the deal belongs to the agent, who works it in their own dashboard. When it sells, they mark it won, and the master dashboard updates live.

- **Types:** site visit, discovery call, consultation, follow-up, closing meeting
- **Lengths:** 30, 45 or 60 minutes
- **Statuses:** pending, confirmed, completed, rescheduled, cancelled
- **After:** feedback, outcome and next steps attached to the lead

## Module: questions answered any hour

The AI support inbox answers buyers, sellers and tenants straight away. If it can't resolve a thread within the escalation window, a person takes over, with the whole conversation in front of them.

- **Categories:** financing, property, scheduling, technical, general
- **Statuses:** open, AI handling, escalated, resolved
- **Escalation:** automatic, after a set number of minutes (default 15)
- **Tracked:** resolution rate, response time, escalation rate, satisfaction
